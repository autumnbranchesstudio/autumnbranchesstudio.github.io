THE CAFÉ AS A LOCAL CHARACTER.

THE OPPORTUNITY

A neighbourhood café concept built around everyday ritual, real people and the energy of being somewhere worth returning to.

THE ROLE OF THE BRAND

Café Lumière should sound and behave like a familiar place with a point of view. Warm, observant, social and rooted in the everyday life around it.

PRINCIPLES

Four rules for making the café feel lived-in rather than generic.

01Local

Talk about the neighbourhood, not just the product.

02Alive

People, movement and real moments over perfect still life.

03Familiar

Build recurring rituals people recognise and return to.

04Specific

Give the café its own language instead of generic coffee captions.

02 / CONTENT SYSTEM

Consistency comes from recurring characters, places and rituals, not from forcing every post into the same template.

CONTENT TERRITORIES

Recurring ways into the category, each with a different job.

01The faces of the place

Staff, regulars, quick conversations, local collaborations and the people who make the café feel lived in.

02The things that repeat

Morning coffee, favourite tables, lunchtime rushes, rainy days and routines people build around the space.

03Coffee with context

Menu stories, seasonal items, ingredient details and product content that still feels like part of the café world.

04What is happening here?

Local events, nearby businesses, street details and reasons to follow even when someone is not buying coffee today.

03 / EXECUTION

The system in use: different formats, same point of view.

TAKEAWAY

MAKE THE PLACE WORTH FOLLOWING.

The concept turns the café into a character people can recognise between visits, not just a product feed.
