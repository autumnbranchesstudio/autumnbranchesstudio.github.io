MARA VALE / CONCEPT STUDY
01 / Point of view

BE INTERESTING BEFORE TRYING TO BE CONSISTENT.

THE IDEA

A concept personal brand built around perspective, cultural curiosity and useful expertise. The goal is to feel like a person worth following, not a content machine.

HOW RECOGNITION IS BUILT

Mara should build recognition through recurring perspective, not identical formats. The audience should know what kind of mind they are following before they know what the next post will look like.

PRINCIPLES

Four rules for building recognition around perspective rather than templates.

01Specific

Have opinions strong enough to sound like one person.

02Cultural

Let books, spaces, film, objects and observation shape the point of view.

03Useful

Expertise still has to help someone think or act differently.

04Human

Show enough life that the authority belongs to a real person.

02 / CONTENT SYSTEM

The content can change shape constantly as long as the perspective remains recognizable.

FOUR TERRITORIES. ONE POINT OF VIEW.

01 / WORKWHAT I KNOW

Decisions, lessons, frameworks.

02 / CULTUREWHAT SHAPES THE THINKING

Books, film, spaces, objects.

03 / OPINIONWHAT I BELIEVE

Arguments with a point of view.

04 / LIFETHE PERSON AROUND THE WORK

Enough context to make authority human.

CONTENT TERRITORIES

Recurring ways into the category, each with a different job.

01What she knows

Projects, decisions, lessons, frameworks and strong perspectives drawn from real expertise.

02What shapes the thinking

Books, film, design, places, objects and references that add texture to the professional point of view.

03What she believes

Direct observations and arguments that create distinction instead of safe agreement.

04The person around the work

Enough context, routines and real moments to make the expertise feel inhabited.

03 / EXECUTION

The system in use: different formats, same point of view.
