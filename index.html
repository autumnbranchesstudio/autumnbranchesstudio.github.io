Process

How I would approach a brand.

This is useful portfolio content because it shows a prospective client that you have a repeatable method.

01 — Diagnose

Review the business, audience, offers, current content, competitors, and gaps.

02 — Position

Choose the audience, brand voice, key messages, content pillars, and channel priorities.

03 — Create

Build a practical calendar with hooks, formats, scripts, captions, visual direction, and CTAs.

04 — Improve

Measure what earns attention and action, then iterate based on reach, saves, clicks, DMs, and leads.
