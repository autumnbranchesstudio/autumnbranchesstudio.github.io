THE PROBLEM

Infrastructure companies often communicate through dense technical language or generic futurism. Verde needs to signal technical depth while still making its value clear to decision-makers who care about performance, reliability and scale.

WHAT WOULD
WE WATCH?

The useful signals are not vanity engagement. We would track whether the right buyers understand the proposition, explore the product and move closer to a commercial conversation.

01 Reach

Visibility among target industries, roles and relevant technical audiences.

02 Depth

Video completion, carousel completion, dwell time and saves on technical content.

03 Intent

Website visits, solution-page clicks, downloads and demo-page activity.

04 Leads

Demo requests, technical enquiries and high-fit conversations influenced by content.

05 Trust

Repeat engagement from technical buyers and continued interaction with deeper content.
