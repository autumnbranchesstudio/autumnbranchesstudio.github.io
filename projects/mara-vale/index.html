THE PROBLEM

Many personal brands become visually polished but emotionally interchangeable. Mara needs a system that builds authority without reducing her into productivity carousels, recycled advice and a perfectly consistent feed.

WHO WOULD
CHOOSE TO
FOLLOW?

People do not need more generic expertise. They follow when the thinking, taste and perspective make the information feel attached to an actual person.

WHO

CURIOUS
PEOPLE.

Founders, creatives and ambitious professionals interested in work, culture, ideas and how people build things.

WHAT THEY WANT

USEFUL
THINKING.

Perspective they can apply, references they would not have found themselves, and expertise without constant instruction.

WHAT THEY IGNORE

CONTENT
FOR CONTENT'S
SAKE.

Empty consistency, obvious engagement bait, recycled founder advice and posting that exists only because the calendar says so.

POSITIONING

BE INTERESTING
BEFORE TRYING
TO BE CONSISTENT.

Mara should build recognition through recurring perspective, not identical formats. The audience should know what kind of mind they are following before they know what the next post will look like.

01 Specific

Have opinions strong enough to sound like one person.

02 Cultural

Let books, spaces, film, objects and observation shape the point of view.

03 Useful

Expertise still has to help someone think or act differently.

04 Human

Show enough life that the authority belongs to a real person.

ONE POINT
OF VIEW.

01 / WORK

WHAT SHE

02 / CULTURE

WHAT SHAPES

03 / OPINION

04 / LIFE

THE PERSON

RECURRING SERIES

THINGS I AM
NOT SURE I
BELIEVE ANYMORE.

AN OPINION SHOULD OPEN A CONVERSATION.
01 / CLAIM Say the thing.

Open with one assumption from the industry that Mara genuinely questions.

02 / ARGUE Explain why.

Use a short video, written post or carousel to develop the idea without turning it into generic education.

03 / INVITE Make room for response.

Ask the audience what they believe, disagree with or have experienced differently.

04 / EXTEND Let it become a series.

Build recognition around the thinking rather than locking the series into one visual template.

ONE WEEK.
SEVEN DIFFERENT
WAYS TO SHOW UP.

The calendar avoids forcing every day into the same content logic. The goal is a recognizable person, not a production schedule people can see from the outside.

MON
Long thought

A deeper written post on a work or industry idea that deserves more than a quick tip.

TUE
Photo dump

Work, objects, places and fragments that reveal taste and context without overexplaining them.

WED
Direct-to-camera

A concise observation delivered in Mara's actual voice with minimal production.

THU
Cultural note

A book, scene, exhibition, place or object connected back to how Mara thinks about work.

FRI
Project breakdown

Show one decision, problem or behind-the-work detail that demonstrates expertise through practice.

SAT
One-line opinion

A short, memorable point of view that can stand on its own and invite conversation.

SUN
Life without lesson

Something human that does not need to become advice, a framework or a professional takeaway.

WHAT WOULD
WE WATCH?

A personal brand should create recognition, trust and opportunity. The important signals show whether the audience knows what Mara stands for and wants more of her thinking.

01 Recognition

Repeat viewers, profile visits and whether recurring ideas become identifiable.

02 Depth

Saves, shares, dwell time and meaningful comments on perspective-led content.

03 Conversation

Replies, DMs and audience responses that create actual exchange.

04 Authority

Invitations, mentions, collaborations and opportunities tied to Mara's expertise.

05 Opportunity

Qualified enquiries, speaking requests, project conversations and long-term relationship building.
