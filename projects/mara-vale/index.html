THE PROBLEM

Many personal brands become polished but interchangeable. Mara needs authority without collapsing into productivity carousels, recycled founder advice and a feed that looks consistent because every post is the same.

WHO WOULD
CHOOSE TO
FOLLOW?

People do not need more generic expertise. They follow when the thinking, taste and perspective feel attached to an actual person.

WHO

CURIOUS
PEOPLE.

Founders, creatives and ambitious professionals interested in work, culture, ideas and how people build things.

WHAT THEY WANT

USEFUL
THINKING.

Perspective they can apply, references they would not have found themselves, and expertise without constant instruction.

WHAT THEY IGNORE

CONTENT FOR
CONTENT'S SAKE.

Empty consistency, obvious engagement bait, recycled founder advice and posting that exists only because the calendar says so.

POSITIONING

BE INTERESTING
BEFORE TRYING
TO BE CONSISTENT.

Mara should build recognition through recurring perspective, not identical formats. The audience should know what kind of mind they are following before they know what the next post will look like.

01Specific

Have opinions strong enough to sound like one person.

02Cultural

Let books, spaces, film, objects and observation shape the point of view.

03Useful

Expertise still has to help someone think or act differently.

04Human

Show enough life that the authority belongs to a real person.

WHAT WOULD
WE WATCH?

The useful question is whether Mara becomes more recognizable for her thinking, and whether that recognition creates qualified attention over time.

01Qualified reach

Visibility among the founders, creatives and professionals the positioning is built for.

02Saves

Signals that the thinking or references are useful enough to return to later.

03Shares

Whether an idea feels specific enough to pass to another person.

04Conversation

Comments, replies and DMs that show the point of view is opening real discussion.

05Intent

Profile visits, site visits and inbound interest after repeated exposure to the content.
